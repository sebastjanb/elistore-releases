Getting set up

A source is only a list. Something still has to sign the apps with your Apple ID and put them on the phone — that is what SideStore does.

  1. Install SideStore

    Follow the setup at docs.sidestore.io. You pair the phone with a computer once, upload the pairing file, and after that SideStore refreshes apps on its own — no computer on the same Wi-Fi, no AltServer running in the background.

  2. Add EliStore

    Open this page on the phone and tap Add to SideStore, or go to Browse → Sources → + and paste the URL above.

  3. Install what you want

    The apps below appear under the source. Tap Free, wait for the install, and trust the certificate in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management the first time.

  4. Keep them alive

    A free Apple ID signs apps for seven days and allows three at a time. SideStore refreshes them in the background before they expire; open it once a week and they never lapse. A paid Apple Developer account raises the limit and the signature lasts a year.

Questions you would reasonably have

Does this work with AltStore too?

Yes. AltStore and SideStore read the same source format. AltStore Classic needs AltServer running on a Mac or PC on the same network to refresh apps; SideStore does not, which is why it is the one recommended here. AltStore PAL is a different thing — an Apple-approved EU marketplace that only accepts notarised apps, so it cannot load this source.

The button did nothing, or the app closed.

The sidestore:// and altstore:// links only work on a phone that already has the app installed. On a desktop browser nothing happens; on a phone without it, the link can close the browser tab. Copy the URL and paste it into the app instead.

Why does the app expire after a week?

Because Apple's free developer certificates last seven days. Nothing here can change that. SideStore re-signs before expiry as long as you open it now and then.

Is any of this account data leaving the phone?

Not through the store. This site serves a JSON file and some images; the IPAs are downloaded from GitHub. Your Apple ID goes to SideStore and to Apple, never here.
